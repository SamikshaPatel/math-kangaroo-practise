## Overview

The Kangaroo Mathematics competition at the Écolier level (Grades 3-4) assesses students across **12 major problem categories** with **35 distinct sub-categories**. Problems emphasize logical reasoning, spatial visualization, and creative problem-solving rather than memorization.

### Distribution Across Papers (2009-2025)
- **Total Papers Analyzed:** 16 years (2009-2025)
- **Total Questions Analyzed:** 384 problems
- **Average Questions Per Year:** 24
- **Consistent Structure:** 8 × 3-point, 8 × 4-point, 8 × 5-point questions
- **Recent Analysis Focus:** 2022-2025 papers (48 questions) showing current trends and emerging patterns

### Key Characteristics
- **Multiple Choice Format:** All answers selected from 5 options (A-E)
- **Visual Components:** ~40% of questions include diagrams, grids, or visual elements
- **Multi-step Reasoning:** Problems often require combining multiple concepts
- **Constraint-Based:** Many problems have multiple conditions to satisfy simultaneously
- **Time-Efficient:** Designed to be solved in 60 minutes with strategic time management

## Complete Question Type Categories

### 1. ARITHMETIC & CALCULATION
**Main Focus:** Basic number operations and numerical problem-solving  
**Frequency in Exam:** ~3-4 questions per year  
**Key Skills:** Mental math, order of operations, number sense

#### 1.1 Simple Arithmetic
- **Description:** Direct arithmetic operations with small numbers
- **Concepts Covered:** Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division
- **Typical Difficulty:** 3-4 points
- **Example:** "2 × 9 + 200 + 9 ="
- **Recent Example (2023 Écolier Q2):** Symbol equation problems where different shapes represent different numerical values that must be solved systematically
- **Practice Focus:** Speed and accuracy with mental calculation
- **Common Mistakes:** Order of operations errors, simple arithmetic errors
- **Prerequisite Skills:** Basic facts (multiplication tables, addition within 20)

#### 1.2 Multi-step Calculations
- **Description:** Combining multiple arithmetic operations in sequence
- **Concepts Covered:** Chained operations, balance scales, weight systems
- **Typical Difficulty:** 4-5 points
- **Example:** "Given weights of different building blocks, find total weight"
- **Practice Focus:** Systematic approach to complex calculations
- **Common Mistakes:** Losing track of information, arithmetic errors mid-sequence
- **Prerequisite Skills:** Confidence with operations, ability to organize information

#### 1.3 Number Patterns & Sequences
- **Description:** Identifying patterns in sequences and completing them
- **Concepts Covered:** Number sequences, digit patterns, consecutive numbers
- **Typical Difficulty:** 3-4 points
- **Example:** "213, 214, 215... What digits are missing in four-digit numbers?"
- **Practice Focus:** Pattern recognition and systematic listing
- **Common Mistakes:** Missing the pattern rule, incorrect extrapolation
- **Prerequisite Skills:** Understanding number relationships, skip counting

### 2. GEOMETRY & SPATIAL REASONING
**Main Focus:** Shape properties, spatial visualization, 2D and 3D reasoning  
**Frequency in Exam:** ~5-6 questions per year  
**Key Skills:** Visualization, spatial orientation, area/perimeter concepts

#### 2.1 2D Shape Analysis
- **Description:** Properties and relationships of flat shapes
- **Concepts Covered:** Shapes (square, rectangle, circle, triangle), area, overlap
- **Typical Difficulty:** 3-5 points
- **Example:** "How many grey tiles are completely covered by a poster?"
- **Recent Examples:** 
  - *2023 Écolier Q4:* Jigsaw pieces forming a square with specific placement requirements
  - *2023 Écolier Q8:* Paper pieces on a circle with coverage/overlap analysis
  - *2023 Écolier Q9:* Shape filling with pieces matching areas
  - *2023 Écolier Q16:* Composite pieces creating geometric patterns
- **Practice Focus:** Counting and visualization with overlapping shapes
- **Common Mistakes:** Incorrect counting of overlaps, misunderstanding coverage
- **Prerequisite Skills:** Shape recognition, counting, area intuition

#### 2.2 Perimeter & Measurement
- **Description:** Calculating perimeters and working with measurements
- **Concepts Covered:** Perimeter formula, proportional relationships, dimensions
- **Typical Difficulty:** 3-4 points
- **Example:** "Rectangle length 8cm, width half as long. Find square side with same perimeter"
- **Practice Focus:** Formula application and relationship understanding
- **Common Mistakes:** Confusing perimeter with area, calculation errors
- **Prerequisite Skills:** Understanding perimeter concept, basic measurement

#### 2.3 3D Objects & Cubes
- **Description:** Visualizing and reasoning about three-dimensional structures
- **Concepts Covered:** Cube stacking, 3D structures, spatial relationships
- **Typical Difficulty:** 3-4 points
- **Example:** "A cat throws a cube off a structure. Which structure was built?"
- **Practice Focus:** Mental 3D rotation and visualization
- **Common Mistakes:** Difficulty visualizing 3D from 2D, miscounting cubes
- **Prerequisite Skills:** Basic cube/3D shape knowledge, spatial reasoning

#### 2.4 Visual Patterns & Transformations
- **Description:** Rotations, reflections, and pattern changes
- **Concepts Covered:** Reflections, rotations, symmetry, perspective changes
- **Typical Difficulty:** 3-4 points
- **Example:** "Black/white squares on window inside. What's seen from outside?"
- **Recent Examples:**
  - *2022 Écolier Q5:* Mirror reflection of laser beam through multiple mirrors
  - *2023 Écolier Q3:* Rotation tracking on disc dials with position changes
  - *2023 Écolier Q6:* Paper folding creating reflection patterns
- **Practice Focus:** Mental rotation and reflection visualization
- **Common Mistakes:** Incorrect direction of reflection/rotation, orientation errors
- **Prerequisite Skills:** Understanding reflection and rotation concepts

#### 2.5 Tiling & Grid Problems
- **Description:** Working with tiling patterns and grid-based geometric problems
- **Concepts Covered:** Tiling patterns, rectangular/square combinations, dimensions
- **Typical Difficulty:** 4-5 points
- **Example:** "Kitchen floor with rectangular (23cm × 11cm) and square tiles"
- **Practice Focus:** Proportion and dimension inference from patterns
- **Common Mistakes:** Incorrect dimension assumptions, calculation errors
- **Prerequisite Skills:** Proportional reasoning, factor relationships

### 3. LOGIC & REASONING PUZZLES
**Main Focus:** Deductive logic, constraint satisfaction, logical inference  
**Frequency in Exam:** ~4-5 questions per year  
**Key Skills:** Logical deduction, systematic approach, constraint management

#### 3.1 Logical Deduction
- **Description:** Using given conditions to systematically deduce answers

- **Example:** "Cards of same shape hide same digits. Different shapes = different digits"
- **Recent Examples:**
  - *2022 Écolier Q15:* Analyzing tournament point possibilities with multiple win/draw/loss scenarios
  - *2023 Écolier Q12:* Spatial logic deducing house positions relative to streets
  - *2023 Écolier Q15:* Graph coloring problem requiring logical deduction
  - *2023 Écolier Q24:* Grid pattern deduction from logical clues
- **Practice Focus:** Systematic constraint checking and variable assignment
- **Common Mistakes:** Overlooking conditions, inconsistent assignments
- **Prerequisite Skills:** Understanding logical conditionals, systematic thinking

#### 3.2 Set & Venn Diagram Problems
- **Description:** Understanding relationships between sets and their intersections
- **Concepts Covered:** Set theory, union/intersection, logical statements
- **Typical Difficulty:** 3-4 points
- **Example:** "Where is Kangaroo: in circle and triangle but not in square?"
- **Practice Focus:** Set relationship understanding and position identification
- **Common Mistakes:** Confusing union/intersection, misinterpreting "and/or/not"
- **Prerequisite Skills:** Understanding set concepts, logical connectors

#### 3.3 Pattern Recognition & Completion
- **Description:** Identifying patterns and predicting next elements
- **Concepts Covered:** Repeating patterns, alternating sequences, modular arithmetic
- **Typical Difficulty:** 3-5 points
- **Example:** "Yellow/green vests alternate, red/brown/purple backpacks pattern"
- **Recent Examples:**
  - *2022 Écolier Q6:* Pattern of jumps on number line identifying sequence rules
  - *2023 Écolier Q14:* Animals arranged in sequence with constraint patterns
  - *2023 Écolier Q22:* Machine sequence transformation with rotation and printing
- **Practice Focus:** Pattern recognition across multiple dimensions
- **Common Mistakes:** Identifying wrong pattern basis, incorrect extrapolation

#### 3.4 Constraint Satisfaction
- **Description:** Finding solutions meeting multiple simultaneous conditions
- **Concepts Covered:** Systems of constraints, variable assignment, logic puzzles
- **Typical Difficulty:** 4-5 points
- **Example:** "7 cards in rings: sum in each ring = 10, find missing number"
- **Recent Examples:**
  - *2023 Écolier Q13:* Cars with people assignment meeting multiple constraints

  - *2023 Écolier Q23:* Numbers in circles where sum between adjacent numbers must satisfy conditions

- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting conditions, arithmetic errors, incomplete checking
- **Prerequisite Skills:** Arithmetic, organizational skills, patience

## Practice Guidelines

### Building a Practice Schedule

#### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-2)
- **Focus:** Mastering question types individually
- **Structure:** One question type per day
- **Quantity:** 3-4 problems from single type
- **Goal:** Understand structure and common approaches
- **Example Day:** Practice only "2D Shape Analysis" problems

#### Phase 2: Mixed Practice (Weeks 3-4)
- **Focus:** Combining question types and increasing speed
- **Structure:** Mix 3-point and 4-point questions
- **Quantity:** 6-8 mixed problems
- **Goal:** Develop pattern recognition across types
- **Example:** 3 different question types, 2-3 problems each

#### Phase 3: Full Exam Simulation (Weeks 5-6)
- **Focus:** Complete 24-question practice under exam conditions
- **Structure:** All three difficulty levels in order
- **Quantity:** Full 60-minute exam
- **Goal:** Time management and pressure resilience
- **Example:** Complete entire paper, track timing per question

#### Phase 4: Targeted Reinforcement (Weeks 7-8)
- **Focus:** Weak areas and hardest question types
- **Structure:** Focused practice on problem areas
- **Quantity:** 10-15 problems from weak categories
- **Goal:** Mastery of difficult concepts

### Common Problem Areas by Sub-Category

#### 2D Shape Analysis
- **Common Mistake:** Double-counting overlapping regions
- **Prevention:** Color or mark different regions clearly
- **Practice Tip:** Draw grid over diagrams to count precisely

#### Constraint Satisfaction
- **Common Mistake:** Forgetting one or more conditions
- **Prevention:** List all constraints before solving
- **Practice Tip:** Verify solution against ALL conditions

#### Combination & Arrangements
- **Common Mistake:** Incomplete enumeration of cases
- **Prevention:** Use systematic listing (organized tree/table)
- **Practice Tip:** Use visualization like branches or organized lists

#### 3D Visualization
- **Common Mistake:** Mental rotation errors
- **Prevention:** Physically manipulate objects while learning
- **Practice Tip:** Build structures with blocks, then visualize changes

#### Multi-step Word Problems
- **Common Mistake:** Missing an operation or using wrong order
- **Prevention:** Write equation/plan before calculating
- **Practice Tip:** Underline key numbers and operations first
