## **Solution Explanations**

**Q1:** The pattern requires 5 hexagons to complete the sequence (Option C).

**Q2:** The right side is a mirror image of the left side, rotated 180° (Option B).

**Q3:** Total = 50 members ÷ 3 = 16.67... ≈ 17 per team
- A: 23 → 17 (move 6)
- B: 19 → 17 (move 2)
- C: 8 → 17 (receive 7)
Actually need to move 7 from A → C (Option C).

**Q5:** 360° ÷ 45° = 8 rotations (Option C).

**Q9:** If 1st → 20 km, 2nd → 15 km, 3rd → 12 km
- 2nd speed ratio: 15/20 = 3/4
- When 2nd finishes 20 km, 3rd completes: 12/15 × 20 = 16 km
- Distance from finish: 20 - 16 = 4 km (Option C)

**Q13:** Calculate individual times:
- 1st: 2:15
- 2nd: 4:32 - 2:15 = 2:17
- 3rd: 6:58 - 4:32 = 2:26
- 4th: 9:17 - 6:58 = 2:19
- 5th: 11:45 - 9:17 = 2:28
Fastest is 4th swimmer at 2:19 (Option D).

**Q18:** 
- Apples: 12 → 18 (gain 6)
- Bananas: 8 → 4 (lose 4)
- Pears: 5 → 3 (lose 2)
If 4 bananas became apples, and 3 apples became bananas, net = 1 gain. But need to solve: Let x = apples→bananas, y = apples→pears
- From bananas: 8 - y + x = 4 → x - y = -4
- From apples: 12 - x - y + (people from B&P) = 18
Solving: x = 3 (Option B).

**Q24:** Solve: 3a + 4b + 5c = 25
- a=5, b=0, c=2: ✓
- a=3, b=4, c=0: ✓
- a=3, b=0, c=2.8: ✗
... (enumerate all solutions)
Total: 8 different bouquets (Option D).

## **Study Tips for Benjamin Level**

1. **Read diagrams carefully** — Most questions involve visual/spatial reasoning
2. **Draw your own diagrams** — Helps solve 3D and logic problems
3. **Test small cases first** — Especially for combinatorics problems
4. **Check constraints** — List all conditions before solving
5. **Work backwards** — Useful for logic puzzles and deduction
6. **Look for patterns** — Math Kangaroo loves sequences and symmetries
7. **Manage time wisely** — Spend ~90 sec on 3-pt, ~2 min on 4-pt, ~3-4 min on 5-pt
8. **Avoid guessing on 5-point questions** — Wrong answer costs you more than leaving blank
