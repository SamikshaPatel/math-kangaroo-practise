## **Study Tips for Students**

1. **Read the question twice** — Make sure you understand what's being asked
2. **Identify the operation** — What mathematical skills does this need?
3. **Show your work** — Even on multiple choice, write down your reasoning
4. **Check your units** — Make sure the answer has correct units (cm, km, m², etc.)
5. **Eliminate obviously wrong answers** — This saves time on hard questions
6. **Manage your time** — Spend less time on 3-point questions, more on 5-point
7. **Look for patterns** — Math Kangaroo loves patterns and sequences
8. **Draw diagrams** — Visual representations help with geometry and logic problems
9. **Double-check calculations** — Especially when combining multiple operations
10. **Review your answers** — If you have time at the end, verify your solutions
